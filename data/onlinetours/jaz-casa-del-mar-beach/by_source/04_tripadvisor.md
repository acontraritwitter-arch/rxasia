### 2. Thebakedhater
- источник: [tripadvisor.co.uk](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Hotel_Review-g297549-d23263857-Reviews-JAZ_Elite_Casa_Del_Mar_Beach-Hurghada_Red_Sea_and_Sinai.html) · оценка: **5.0** · опубликован: Mar 2025 · визит: March 2025

**Fabulous hotel with top quality service!**

After being recommended Jaz hotels by a friend who's been to Hurghada many times, we settled on the Jaz Casa Del Mar. I have to admit that the absence of a huge water park was one of the deciding factors, coupled with the beachfront location!
This was our first visit to Egypt, so there was some apprehension of what to expect.
On arrival we were greeted by security on the gate, followed by a short drive/walk up to the reception. First impressions were excellent! The greeting was warm and friendly and the porters took care of our cases. We were presented with cold towels and a cold drink which was very much appreciated.
Our room was modern, spacious and had a sea view balcony. Tea/coffee facilities in room alongside mini fridge, and TV catering for a range of languages as well as live football matches and movies.
All inclusive dining was of a very high standard. Dishes were always varied and catered to all tastes. The daily BBQ featured shawarma, lamb chops, marinated chicken and beef as well as fresh fish.
There were always plenty of salads,fruits, breads and cheeses to choose from and we never had a bad meal. The speciality restaurants are well worth a visit with some excellent food on offer and all part of the all inclusive package. Premium dishes, such as fresh shellfish and seafood platters did incur an extra charge if you fancied something a little more special.
Drinks are local brands and the beers wines and spirits were all perfectly drinkable with little or no difference from home brands.
We drank the hotel water and had ice in our drinks and never had any problems whatsoever.
Staff are hard working, very friendly and totally professional at all times, and were attentive without being intrusive.
The complex was maintained to an extremely high standard and was absolutely spotless at all times. There were always maintenance people tending to the gardens and carrying out small jobs around the hotel and grounds, but never causing inconvenience.
There's a choice of pools with the beach side pool being most popular. As with all hotels, there were of course the early morning sunbed reserving usuals, but we always found free sunbeds and sun shades on the beach, which also has a small bar and waiter service. The adult pool and the pool in the centre of the complex were always a little quieter with sunbeds always available.
Location wise, it's not right in the centre of Hurghada but still close enough to not feel isolated. Outside of the hotel, there's a selection of local shops, restaurants and bars if you fancy something different.
We used Uber cabs to get into town and were never charged more that £2 GBP...but a small tip was always greatly appreciated by the drivers.
I would happily return to the Jaz Casa Del Mar, we considered it an excellent choice for our first stay in Egypt, and would happily recommend it to friends and family.

A really lovely hotel set in an enviable position. The hotel is really well appointed and the staff work tirelessly to make your stay enjoyable. After a short transfer from the airport we received a warm welcome and a complimentary, drink while we were being checked in. Wristbands are provided and they are very strict on checking these when you leave and re-enter the hotel and grounds which is very reassuring in terms of security. The food is great and the drinks are plentiful. The main pool in front of the beach is extremely busy and seems to be where most of the activity happens during the day (you'll need to get up early to secure a cabana in this location), but if you prefer something a little quieter, there are other pools surrounded by lush planting nearby all with white shady cabanas and a bar and snack service.
The rooms are large and bright and housekeeping visited every day. You are supplied with one bottle of water each day in the room, but I realised if you left a note on the pad supplied (and a euro or two), you could request more water. There are also other soft drinks in the mini bar which is restocked daily too. Clean beach towels are provided every day if necessary if you hang the towel bag on your room door.
The staff were all so wonderful, always smiling and helpful. There are too many to mention but we would like to thank Hamedy, Tony 'Macaroni', Wassa, and Karim at the Splash Bar pool to name but a few.
There are three al a carte restaurants that can be booked during your visit. Ocars, Pomodoro and Aqua. In our opinion Pomodoro was the best and Michael our host was delightful, the food was delicious. Oscars was ok, the meat a little overcooked on the night perhaps, and Aqua rated 3rd place. The buffet restaurant had a great selection to suit all tastes and it was never boring or bland. Although the drinks are included as part of the AI package, I would not recommend the white or red wine, however the rose is passable/drinkable.
If you are looking for a relaxing week away this is definitely one to choose, I didn't speak to anyone who didn't like it. However, if you are looking for nightlife and nightly shows to entertain you, you will be disappointed. And in my opinion the only thing lacking in this otherwise wonderful hotel.
Did I enjoy my holiday? 100% yes. Would I return? 100% yes.
A massive thank you to ALL the wonderful staff who made our stay so very enjoyable for the 7 nights were stayed at the Jaz Casa del Mar Beach.
